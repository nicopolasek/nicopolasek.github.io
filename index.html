Nicolas Polasek

Welcome! I am a PhD student at the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics at the University of California, Berkeley.

I am on the 2026-2027 job market.

My research is in development and labor economics, with a focus on how households and communities respond to shocks, labor market frictions, and social protection programs in Sub-Saharan Africa. Methodologically, I use experimental and quasi-experimental methods to study how markets, social networks, and policy interventions shape economic well-being.
