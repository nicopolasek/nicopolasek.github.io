Welcome! I am a PhD student at the Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics at the University of California, Berkeley.

My primary field is development economics and my secondary field is labor economics. My ongoing research projects focus on Sub-Saharan Africa and address topics related to displacement, poverty, and structural change. I use experimental and quasi-experimental methods to document how households and individuals respond to shocks, and to generate evidence on policies that can create opportunities for improved economic well-being.
